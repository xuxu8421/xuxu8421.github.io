RESEARCH THE OPPORTUNITY

Start with both the technology and the person using it.

I reviewed 50+ studies and industry solutions around remote photoplethysmography (rPPG), which estimates physiological signals from camera input. This helped frame the capabilities and constraints of consumer cameras alongside potential applications.

I interviewed six users and built a community of more than 100 members. The interviews and subsequent feedback gave the team a way to examine user needs alongside technical feasibility.

MAKE IT TESTABLE

Turn the research into an app people can try.

I wrote the app PRD, worked on UI improvements, and supported iOS development and internal and external testing. My contribution was product definition and testing support; the work connected research findings to requirements the team could discuss and implement.

REVISIT THE DIRECTION

Ask what the feedback means for the business model.

Repeated-use and willingness-to-pay feedback prompted a closer look at the consumer subscription approach. I organized five application scenarios and explored a path toward B2B2C delivery, where the technology could sit within a broader service.

What I took from this work

Technical feasibility and product demand need separate evidence. Writing the PRD and helping test the app made it possible to bring user feedback back into the product direction.
